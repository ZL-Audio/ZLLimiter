ATH A noise-shaper reference

Noise shaper coefficients for SSRC, written by Naoki Shibata ( shibatch@users.sourceforge.net )
Source: https://github.com/shibatch/SSRC/blob/d76e76d0904ca491b1829b6b0b36fcedb24547df/src/include/shibatch/shapercoefs.h
Revision: d76e76d0904ca491b1829b6b0b36fcedb24547df
Reference license: BSL-1.0

The parameters in ath_a.hpp are a new numerical approximation of the
48 kHz ATH Curve A, intensity 1 noise-transfer magnitude. The reference is
continued at its 24 kHz endpoint above 24 kHz, then normalized at each
sample rate to give a minimum-phase noise transfer with direct term one.
Three Ivantsov sections were fitted jointly at 48, 96 and 192 kHz. These
parameters are not the native SSRC A1 presets at other sample rates.
The original reference notice and license are retained below; the new
ZLLimiter implementation remains under the project's AGPL-3.0 license.

The existing Ivantsov coefficient implementation is described in:
Yuriy Ivantsov, On the Ideal Bilinear and Biquadratic Digital Filter.
https://ivantsovy.com/research/paper1.pdf

Noise-transfer normalization and strictly causal feedback:
Hans Georg Brachtendorf and Christoph Dalpiaz,
Near-optimal Multiplier-Less Broadband Noise Shaping Filters (2023), section 2.
https://doi.org/10.1007/s11265-023-01889-1
